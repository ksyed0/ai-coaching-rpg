**Revision 2 (2026-11-08):** revised after an independent review of revision 1; every finding was checked against the code and is answered in "Review responses" at the end (applied, where, or rejected with evidence). The design changes of this revision: **one LiveKit room per seat** (not per session), a per-frame **generation** check in the worker, the worker **asks** control for its LiveKit token when it is about to connect, a separate `voice_availability` parent message, a **lease** on the global speech-to-text slots, a `./livekit` **subpath export** of `@acr/adapters`, restart budget **5 consecutive failures** reset after 60 s ready, and notice v3's draft marker **kept** until the release gate.

**Goal:** A participant in the browser chooses "take part with voice", checks the microphone in the lobby, joins their seat's LiveKit room with a short-lived microphone-only token, can mute, and hears (reads) private voice notices; the speech worker subscribes only to current recording holders and attributes every frame to the seat generation it came from, restarts with bounded backoff, polls Whisper health and shares one leased speech-to-text budget across sessions; the facilitator sees who speaks and fixed voice alerts. Still dark behind `VOICE_ENABLED=0`.

**Architecture:** Control mints LiveKit tokens through a new adapter in `packages/adapters/src/livekit` (imported only as `@acr/adapters/livekit`) and serves them on `POST /sessions/:id/voice-token` (service token); the BFF calls it for the cookie's own seat and returns only the token, its expiry and the public `wss` URL. Each human seat of a session has **its own room** (`v` + 32 random hex per seat, in control memory), so a participant's LiveKit connection never shows them anything about another seat. Control derives each seat's LiveKit identity from the runtime's `voice_seats` (role plus a seat generation = an HMAC of the holder's participant id under a per-session key held in memory) and removes stale identities from the seat's room. The worker asks for a per-room token (`livekit_token_request` → `livekit_join`) whenever it is about to connect a seat's room, joins with auto-subscribe off, subscribes only to the identity whose generation is current for a recording holder in the last `voice_state`, and drops any frame whose generation is no longer current, frame by frame. The runtime adds "started", "not muted" and "voice available" to `recording`, a `voice_mute` player message, a facilitator-only `voice`/`speaking` roster field and `voice_unavailable` notices driven by control's `voice_availability`. The web app forwards `joined.voice`, relays `voice_opt_in`/`voice_opt_out`/`voice_mute`, forwards `voice_notice` as a named event, and renders the voice choice, mic check, mute and the console's speaking indicator; the page tears its room connection down on every final stream event.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, pnpm workspaces, Node 22, zod 4 strict schemas, Vitest (jsdom for components), Playwright 1.64 with Chromium fake media devices, Axe. New dependencies (exact pins): `livekit-server-sdk` 2.19.2 (`packages/adapters`, subpath `./livekit` only), `@livekit/rtc-node` 1.2.1 (`services/speech`), `livekit-client` 2.23.0 (`apps/web`).

## Global Constraints

- Every A1a invariant holds: **no change to the session log format**, `LOG_FORMAT`, the consent file or any event; `VOICE_ENABLED` defaults to `0`; **privacy invariant** (audio of a seat that is not the current human holder who chose voice, started, not paused, not ended, not muted, with voice available, is never subscribed, segmented, transcribed or turned into a message, and a frame is attributed only to the seat generation whose track produced it), enforced in the worker (subscription, per-frame generation check in the ingest, generation in the gate) and the runtime (refusal table, `isRecordingHolder`).
- Never log a token, a LiveKit key or secret, a room name, an identity, a participant id, a transcript line, a URL from the environment or a path; log fixed sentences and error names only. This covers library logs: rtc-node's logger is set to error (or silent) and `RUST_LOG` stays out of the speech allowlist (R26); livekit-client runs with `setLogLevel("warn")`. Errors returned to the browser are the BFF's own sentences.
- Token grant (spec 9, overview 7), exactly: `ttl` 600 s; `roomJoin: true`, `canPublish: true`, `canPublishSources: ["microphone"]`, `canSubscribe: false`, `canPublishData: false`, `canUpdateOwnMetadata: false`; room = the seat's own room, `v` + 32 hex per (session, role) in control memory; identity `<roleId>.g<8 hex>`. Worker grant, per seat room: `roomJoin: true`, `canPublish: false`, `canSubscribe: true`, `canPublishData: false`, `canUpdateOwnMetadata: false`, `hidden: true`, identity `SPEECH.<8 hex>`, ttl 600 s, minted fresh on each `livekit_token_request`.
- The browser receives only `{ token, url, expiresAt }` for its own seat; identity comes only from the sealed cookie (never from a body).
- The ingest never uses a track name, a participant name, metadata or attributes: only the identity (parsed), the track kind (`audio`) and the source (`microphone`), and at most one subscribed track per identity.
- Constants (code review only): restart backoff base 500 ms, doubling (500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000), 5 consecutive failures, reset after `RESTART_STABLE_MS` 60 000 ready; `STT_HEALTH_POLL_MS` 5000; `STT_GLOBAL_IN_FLIGHT` 2; slot lease `STT_COLD_TIMEOUT_MS + 5000`; `VOICE_TOKEN_TTL_S` 600; BFF voice token throttle 20 per 60 s per participant; `VOICE_SEND_WAIT_MS` 2000; `LIVEKIT_JOIN_WAIT_MS` 10 000 (worker waits for its `livekit_join`); `SEATS_WAIT_MS` 1000 (control waits for a seats report on a token request).
- Strict zod schemas (`strictObject`); parent-channel copies equal (drift test); the worker protocol has one source (`@acr/speech/protocol`); no new regular expression with a letters-digits-underscore class outside `packages/events/src/ids.ts` (US-0020 guard): identities are split on the last `.` and checked with `isScenarioId`.
- Tests: no real-time sleeps, no absolute time assertions, injected `SetTimer`/clocks, private temp directories, `vi.setConfig({ testTimeout: 90_000, hookTimeout: 90_000 })` for real-process and real-socket tests, generous waits for anything that loads a model; e2e with no retries and explicit limits.

## Review Focus

1. **A published track of a seat that is not recording** (text-only, before the start, paused, muted, voice unavailable, a stale identity, an identity that does not parse, a second microphone track, a non-microphone track): never subscribed, no frame, no STT call. [Task 7 `an unconsented published track is never subscribed`, `paused or ended gives an empty allowed set`, `a second microphone track of the same identity is not subscribed`; Task 3 `recording is false before the start`, `a line arriving after mute is refused`]
2. **A reissue or rebind while the old browser is still in the seat's room:** the old identity is removed, the new holder gets a new identity, and no frame of the old identity, not even one already queued, is attributed to the new person. [Task 5 `a new holder for a role removes the old identity`; Task 7 `frames queued after unsubscribe are never delivered`, `two tracks of one role never interleave`; Task 7 `TrackPipeline drops a frame whose generation is not the gate's`]
3. **A participant who reloads the page or loses the stream for a moment:** not kicked from the room by control, the worker stops listening while the seat has no holder and resumes when the same participant rejoins; a leave, a withdrawal or an AI fill does remove. [Task 5 `a plain disconnect removes no identity`, `leave, withdraw and AI fill remove`; Task 7 `a holder that comes back is subscribed again`]
4. **Whisper or the worker failing for long:** bounded consecutive restarts, then voice off with ONE alert, pages say "use the text box", typed lines keep working, no alert storm; the voice turn closes on every worker exit; the global STT slots are leased and cannot be held by a stuck worker. [Task 8, Task 9, Task 6 `an expired lease is reclaimed`]
5. **A page served over plain `http://` on a LAN address, a browser that refuses the microphone, or a web server without LiveKit:** the page says why in words and keeps the text box; nothing retries in a loop; the page leaves the room on every final stream event. [Task 14 `insecure context`, `voiceTransport false`; Task 15 `a denied permission`, `every final event leaves the room`]
