Before you join this practice session

This is a practice conversation for coaching. Please read this short notice before you take your seat.

What is recorded: everything said in the session (your typed lines and the replies), the display name you choose, your role, and the times things happen. This record is used to run the session and to prepare feedback afterwards.

Who sees what: the other participants see the display name you choose and what you say in the scenes you share with them. In this version, only you (the participant) and the facilitator see your feedback, and only the facilitator sees the whole session record.

AI roles: some of the other characters in the scene may be played by an AI, not by a person. Their lines are generated by a computer model and may be wrong or surprising. To produce them, the conversation may be sent to an AI model service.

Please do not share private details about real people, health information or anything you would not want written down.

How to leave: you can stop at any time, without giving a reason. Before the session starts, use the Leave button (or type /withdraw in the terminal) to give up your seat. Once it has started, simply close the page or the terminal. What was said before you left stays in the record.

By selecting "I agree", you confirm that you have read this notice and want to take part.
