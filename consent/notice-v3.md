Before you join this practice session

This is a practice conversation for coaching. Please read this short notice before you take your seat.

What is recorded: everything said in the session (your typed or spoken lines and the replies), the display name you choose, your role, and the times things happen. This record is used to run the session and to prepare feedback afterwards.

Who sees what: the other participants see the display name you choose and what you say in the scenes you share with them. In this version, only you (the participant) and the facilitator see your feedback, and only the facilitator sees the whole session record.

AI roles: some of the other characters in the scene, and some of the other player roles, may be played by an AI, not by a person. A player role played by an AI is always named “AI player” and its lines are marked AI. AI lines are generated by a computer model and may be wrong or surprising. To produce them, the conversation may be sent to an AI model service.

Voice: you choose how to take part. You can take part with voice, or take part by text only; the text box always works, and you can change your choice during the session. If you take part with voice, what you say is converted to text live on the host computer and appears under your name in the record, like a typed line. Your voice is also recorded, and only the facilitator can hear the recording; the other participants, the AI characters and the feedback never receive your audio. The audio is kept until the facilitator deletes it, and it is deleted automatically the first time the application runs after 30 days. You can ask the facilitator to delete your audio at any time. Deletion applies to the session folder on the host computer, not to backups made of that computer. Please use a headset in a private place: other people nearby may be recorded too.

Please do not share private details about real people, health information or anything you would not want written down.

How to leave: you can stop at any time, without giving a reason. Before the session starts, use the Leave button (or type /withdraw in the terminal) to give up your seat. Once it has started, simply close the page or the terminal. What was said before you left stays in the record.

By selecting "I agree", you confirm that you have read this notice and want to take part.
